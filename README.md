# Midterm-Project-Econometric-Foundation
## Research question
How are health spending per person and urbanization associated with life expectancy at birth across countries and economies in 2019, after accounting for GDP per person?

#Data source 
Four World Bank World Development Indicators series for 2019: 
- Life expectancy at birth: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.DYN.LE00.IN?locations=CO
- GDP per capita: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GDP.PCAP.PP.KD
- Current health expenditure per capita: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.XPD.CHEX.PP.CD
- Urban population: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.URB.TOTL.IN.ZS

# How to run 
Open midterm project.ipynb in Google Colab, then Run-all. 

# Headline finding 
Among 186 economies with complete data, doubling health spending per person is associated with 1.35 more years of life expectancy after accounting for GDP and urbanization (95% confidence interval: 0.12 to 2.58 years). This is an association, not a causal effect.
